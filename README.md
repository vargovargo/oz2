# OZ 2.0 — State-Specific Resource

A state-by-state resource for local planners, EDD staff, and community
development directors trying to figure out which Opportunity Zone 2.0
tracts in their jurisdiction are eligible and how to organize a nomination
coalition before the July 1, 2026 nomination window opens.

## Why this exists

The 2025 OBBBA made Opportunity Zones permanent and reset the map. Treasury
released its eligible-tract list April 6, 2026. Governors will nominate up
to 25% of eligible tracts during a 90-day window opening July 1, 2026, with
new designations taking effect January 1, 2027.

There is good national-level coverage from EIG, Novogradac, and OZ industry
press. There is almost no resource that helps a planner in a rural-eligible
county figure out (a) which tracts they have to work with, (b) who to
convene to make the nomination case, (c) how OZ stacks with other federal
rural development capital, and (d) how the rural-area enhanced incentives
(QROFs) change the calculus.

This is that resource.

## Status

In active development. Phase 1 MVP target: all 50 states + DC, shipping
before mid-June 2026.
